Admissions

Take the first step towards your future at Kowa Bunga University.

Admission Requirements

Applicants must meet the minimum entry requirements for their chosen programme. Some programmes may have additional subject or academic requirements.

Academic Requirements

  • Successful completion of Year 12 or an equivalent secondary school qualification.
  • Applicants should meet the minimum academic entry score for their selected programme.
  • Some programmes may require specific subjects such as Mathematics or Science.

English Language Requirements

  • Applicants must demonstrate an appropriate level of English language proficiency.
  • International applicants may be required to provide recognised English language test results.

Required Documents

Applicants should prepare the following documents before submitting an application:

1. Identification

A certified copy of a valid passport, identity document or other approved form of identification.

2. Academic Records

Copies of your most recent school results, certificates or academic transcripts.

3. Proof of English Proficiency

International applicants may need to submit recognised English language test results.

4. Supporting Documents

Certain programmes may require additional documents such as a portfolio, personal statement or supporting certificates.

Application Process

Applying to Kowa Bunga University is simple. Follow the steps below to complete your application.

1

Choose a Programme

Explore our faculties and select the programme that best matches your interests and career goals.

2

Check the Requirements

Review the admission requirements for your selected programme and make sure you meet the entry criteria.

3

Prepare Your Documents

Gather your identification, academic records and any other documents required for your application.

4

Submit Your Application

Complete the university application form and submit all required supporting documents.

5

Application Review

The admissions team will review your application and confirm whether all requirements have been met.

6

Receive Your Outcome

Successful applicants will receive an offer with information about enrolment and the next steps required before starting their studies.
