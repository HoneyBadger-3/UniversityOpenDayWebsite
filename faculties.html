Our Faculties

Explore the study opportunities available at Kowa Bunga University and discover a career path that suits your interests and ambitions.

Faculty of Science

The Faculty of Science provides students with opportunities to explore the natural and applied sciences through a combination of theoretical knowledge and practical laboratory experience.

Students develop problem-solving, research and analytical skills while studying scientific concepts and their applications in the modern world.

Areas of Study

  • Biological Sciences
  • Chemistry
  • Environmental Science
  • Computer Science

Career Opportunities

  • Laboratory Scientist
  • Environmental Scientist
  • Research Assistant
  • Data Analyst
  • Software Developer

Faculty of Commerce

The Faculty of Commerce prepares students for careers in business, finance, management and entrepreneurship.

Our programmes combine business theory with practical skills to help students understand modern organisations and the economic environment in which they operate.

Areas of Study

  • Accounting
  • Business Management
  • Marketing
  • Finance

Career Opportunities

  • Accountant
  • Financial Analyst
  • Marketing Coordinator
  • Business Manager
  • Entrepreneur

Faculty of Engineering

The Faculty of Engineering focuses on developing practical and technical skills that allow students to design solutions to real-world problems.

Students have opportunities to participate in practical projects, laboratory activities and collaborative design challenges throughout their studies.

Areas of Study

  • Civil Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Software Engineering

Career Opportunities

  • Civil Engineer
  • Mechanical Engineer
  • Electrical Engineer
  • Software Engineer
  • Project Engineer
